1. Overview

Rinder is a housing and roommate discovery service. This statement explains what information we may collect, why we use it, when it may be shared and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect your name, email address, authentication information, profile details and preferences you choose to provide.

Housing and roommate activity

We may process listings you view, waitlists you join, roommate profiles you interact with, messages you send and household features you use.

Technical information

We may collect device, browser, IP address, diagnostic and usage information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

3. How we use information

  • Provide listings, roommate discovery, messaging and shared-home features.
  • Personalize suggested listings or people based on information and activity available to the service.
  • Maintain account security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems.
  • Measure product performance and improve usability.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information as a business model. Information may be shared with service providers that help host, secure, analyze or support Rinder; with other users when a feature is designed to show profile, listing, waitlist or message information; or when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. Retention periods may differ by data type.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of certain personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also update profile information through your account when those controls are available.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect account credentials and report suspicious activity.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact the Rinder team through the support channel published in the product. Before public launch, replace this placeholder with your official privacy contact and business address.
